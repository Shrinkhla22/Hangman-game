GAME GUIDE

How to Play

Learn the rules in three simple steps.

1

Choose a Category

Select a category such as Animals, Food, Movies or Technology.

2

Guess Letters

Choose letters to reveal the hidden word. Every wrong guess adds to your attempts.

3

Complete the Word

Guess the complete word before all six wrong attempts are used.
